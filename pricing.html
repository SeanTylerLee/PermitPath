Fleet codes

A company code unlocks one device without an App Store or Google Play subscription. The driver enters it under Settings → Subscription → Company access. That device gets unlimited waypoints and Texas TxPROS import for the paid period.

1–20 codes are $3.99 per code per month. 21 or more are $3.39 per code per month (15% off), and that rate applies to the whole purchase. Pay ahead for 1 month, 6 months, or 1 year. Charges show as STL Apps LLC.
