FAQ

Questions drivers ask

What is PermitPath?

PermitPath is a trucker navigation app for oversize and overweight permit routing. You build a route with unlimited waypoints that matches your permit, then navigate that exact route on iOS or Android.

How much does PermitPath cost?

PermitPath is $3.99 per month. That includes unlimited waypoints, route saving, navigation, and sharing on iPhone and Android.

Does PermitPath work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. PermitPath is available on iOS and Android. You can plan a route on one device and navigate or share it on another.

Can I add every waypoint from my permit?

Yes. PermitPath does not cap waypoints. Add the start, end, and every required turn or control point from the permit so the route in the app matches the route on paper.

Is PermitPath related to PermitPass?

No. PermitPath (permitpathnav.com) is a separate app for oversize load navigation and permit-approved truck routing. It is built for heavy-haul drivers who need custom routes with unlimited waypoints on iOS and Android.
