Texas oversize permits, oversize loads, and Permit Nav

PermitPath is the Permit Path app at permitpathnav.com. Drivers also search for it as Permit Nav. It is for trucking: Texas oversize permits, overweight permits, and oversize loads. It is not a building-permit or parking-permit site.

Have a Texas permit already? Upload the PDF or scan the QR code and PermitPath imports the official TxPROS route from the State of Texas. Need the permit itself? Apply with the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles in TxPROS. PermitPath does not sell or issue permits. It navigates the route on the permit you already have.

PermitPath runs on iPhone, iPad, and Android, follows the official Texas route, and also lets you build a custom oversize route with unlimited waypoints for $3.99 a month.

FAQ

Common questions about PermitPath

What is PermitPath?

PermitPath, also called Permit Path and Permit Nav, is a trucker navigation app for oversize and overweight loads. You import a Texas permit or build a permit route with unlimited waypoints, then follow that route on iOS or Android. It lives at permitpathnav.com and is owned by STL Apps LLC.

Does PermitPath work for Texas oversize permits?

Yes. After TxDMV issues your Texas oversize or overweight permit through TxPROS, upload the PDF or scan the QR code in PermitPath. The app imports the official TxPROS route from the State of Texas so you can navigate that exact path. PermitPath does not sell or issue Texas permits.

What is Permit Nav?

Permit Nav is another name people use for PermitPath because the website is permitpathnav.com. PermitPath, Permit Path, and Permit Nav all refer to the same oversize permit navigation app.

Can PermitPath navigate oversize loads outside Texas?

Yes. You can build a custom oversize or overweight permit route with unlimited waypoints and share it between iOS and Android. Official one-tap import of the state-issued route is available for Texas TxPROS permits.
