FAQ

Common questions about PermitPath

What is PermitPath?

PermitPath is a trucker navigation app for oversize and overweight loads. Build a permit route manually in any state with unlimited waypoints, or import an official Texas TxPROS route from a PDF or QR. It runs on iOS and Android at permitpathnav.com and is owned by STL Apps LLC.

Does PermitPath work outside Texas?

Yes. In any state, build the route yourself by dropping the waypoints from your permit, then navigate that path on iOS or Android. Texas permit import is an extra feature for Texas TxPROS permits. It is not the only way to use the app.

Does PermitPath work for Texas oversize permits?

Yes. After TxDMV issues your Texas oversize or overweight permit through TxPROS, upload the PDF or scan the QR code in PermitPath. The app imports the official TxPROS route from the State of Texas so you can navigate that exact path. PermitPath does not sell or issue Texas permits.

Is Texas import the only way to use PermitPath?

No. Most trips use a route you build manually from your permit. Texas PDF or QR import is available when you have a Texas TxPROS permit. Everywhere else, and anytime you prefer, you pin the route yourself and navigate it.
