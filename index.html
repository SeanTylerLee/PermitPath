How drivers use it

Import from supported states. Build it by hand anywhere else

PermitPath is the oversize permit routing app at permitpathnav.com. When your state is supported, upload the permit PDF or scan the QR and the route the state issued goes straight onto the map. Texas, Wyoming, North Dakota, Colorado, Virginia, Georgia, and Kentucky are all in the app now. Everywhere else, build the route from your permit with pins, navigate it on iPhone, iPad, or Android, and share it with your team. See how it works.

PermitPath does not sell or issue permits. Those come from each state’s permit office, such as TxDMV through TxPROS in Texas. Always compare the route in the app with your permit before you roll. The permit is the legal authority.

FAQ

Common questions about PermitPath

What is PermitPath?

PermitPath is a trucker navigation app for oversize and overweight loads. Build a permit route in any state and navigate it on iOS and Android. Free includes a start pin, an end pin, and up to 4 waypoints. The $3.99 monthly plan removes the waypoint cap and adds state permit import from a PDF or QR. PermitPath is owned by STL Apps LLC.

Which states can I import a permit from?

Texas permits import from the TxPROS PDF or QR code. Wyoming, North Dakota, Colorado, Virginia, Georgia, and Kentucky permits import from the permit PDF. In any other state, build the route yourself by dropping the waypoints from your permit, then navigate it on iOS or Android.

Does PermitPath work for Texas oversize permits?

Yes. After TxDMV issues your Texas oversize or overweight permit through TxPROS, upload the PDF or scan the QR code in PermitPath. The app imports the TxPROS route the State of Texas issued so you can navigate it. Always compare it with your permit before you drive. PermitPath does not sell or issue Texas permits.

Do I need permit import to use PermitPath?

No. In any state, and anytime you prefer, you can pin the route yourself from your permit and navigate it. Import is there when your permit comes from a supported state.
