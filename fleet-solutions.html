Fleet Solutions

Buy company codes for phones and tablets your drivers use. One code unlocks one device. The office pays Permit Path. Drivers never buy the app on the device.

Before you create an account

How Fleet Solutions works

1

Create a fleet account

Sign up below with your company name, office email, and a password. This is a company portal, not an individual driver login.

2

Pay for the codes you need

After you sign in, choose how many codes and how long (1, 6, or 12 months). Pay by credit or debit card on the account page. The charge shows as STL Apps LLC.

3

Get your codes

Purchased codes appear on your fleet account as soon as payment completes. You can save them or email them from that page. Free trial codes (up to 5) are added each evening after you request them.

4

Hand each code to a driver

Give one open code to each driver. In Permit Path they open Settings → Subscription → Company access and enter the code. That device unlocks unlimited waypoints without an App Store or Google Play purchase.

What you need to know

Why companies buy codes

On a personal phone, drivers subscribe through Apple or Google. On a company iPad or Android tablet, the business account often blocks those purchases. Fleet codes solve that. The office buys and pays here. The driver only enters a code in the app.

One code = one device

Each code works on a single phone or tablet. Once a driver enters it, that code is tied to that device and shows as Active · In use. Do not share one code across multiple devices. Codes turned off in Permit Path stay on your list as Off and will not unlock a device.

New or replacement devices

If you issue a driver a new phone or tablet, the old code stays on the old device. Request a new code for the replacement. Contact Sean at 580-729-8184 or support@permitpathnav.com and a new code can be issued.

How payment works

You pay by card through Stripe on your fleet account page. Billing is prepaid for the months you choose—not an automatic monthly charge that renews forever. Each purchase gives those codes their own expiry date, so codes bought on different days can expire on different days. Rates are $3.99 per code per month for 1–20 codes, and $3.39 per code per month (15% off) when you buy 21 or more. The lower rate applies to the whole purchase. Card statements show STL Apps LLC, the parent company of Permit Path.

After you sign in

Your fleet account lists every code, shows Open or In use, and lets you buy more. Use the Help button on that page anytime for a full walkthrough of buying, distributing, and replacing codes.
