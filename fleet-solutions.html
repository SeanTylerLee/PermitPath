How Fleet Solutions works

1

Create a fleet account

Sign up with the company name, office email, and a password.

2

Pay for the codes you need

Choose 1, 6, or 12 months and pay by card. The charge shows as STL Apps LLC.

Purchased codes appear as soon as payment completes. Trial codes appear on the account as soon as you request them.

4

Hand each code to a driver

In Permit Path they open Settings → Subscription → Company access and enter one code. That device unlocks.

What you need to know

  • One code unlocks one phone or tablet. Do not share a code across devices.
  • The office pays here. The driver only types the code in the app.
  • Billing is prepaid for 1, 6, or 12 months. There is no automatic forever-renewing charge. Codes bought on different days can expire on different days.

Rates are $3.99 per code per month for 1–20 codes, and $3.39 per code per month (15% off) when you buy 21 or more. The lower rate applies to the whole purchase.

More about payment, replacements, and volume rates

Why companies buy codes

On a personal phone, drivers subscribe through Apple or Google. On a company iPad or Android tablet, the business account often blocks those purchases. Fleet codes solve that. The office buys and pays here. The driver only enters a code in the app.

New or replacement devices

If you issue a driver a new phone or tablet, open that code on your fleet account and replace it. The old code turns off. A new code with the same expiry appears on the same page.

How payment works

You pay by card through Stripe on your fleet account page. Each purchase gives those codes their own expiry date. Card statements show STL Apps LLC, the parent company of Permit Path.

More discounted pricing

Need a deeper discount than the rates above? Email support@permitpathnav.com. Fleet rates are negotiable. Any agreed rate has to stay above Permit Path’s cost for API, map, navigation, and route-building fees.
