Fleet pricing

The rate depends on how many paid devices the company has. At 21 devices, every device drops to $3.39.

Paid devices Rate Monthly total

1–20 devices stay at $3.99 each. 21 or more are $3.39 each, on every device. After you sign in you can pay 1, 3, 6, or 12 months. A 6-month bill is the monthly total times 6. One code is one phone or tablet. A personal App Store or Google Play subscription stays $3.99/month.
