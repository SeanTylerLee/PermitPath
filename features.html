Features

Build the permit route. Drive it.

PermitPath is built for oversize and overweight hauls—not passenger-car GPS. Match the route on your permit, then navigate it on iPhone or Android.

Step 1

Unlimited waypoints

Long permits have a lot of turns. Add the start, the end, and every required control point. PermitPath does not cap how many waypoints you can put on a route.

Step 2

Navigate the path you built

Turn-by-turn guidance follows the route you entered—not a car GPS guess at the fastest path. Hit Go and stay on the roads the permit allows.

Step 3

Share across iOS & Android

Plan in the office on one phone and send the same route to the truck. PermitPath works on both platforms so the team stays on one path.
