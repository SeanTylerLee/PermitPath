Why PermitPath

Stay on the route you already chose

Free includes a start pin, an end pin, and up to 4 waypoints. The $3.99 monthly plan removes the waypoint cap and adds Texas TxPROS import. On the road, PermitPath stays on that route.

On the road

What the drive actually does

No reroute

PermitPath does not send you around traffic or an accident. It stays on the route you built or the route you imported.

Warns if you leave the line

If the truck leaves the route, the app tells you so you can get back on the permitted path.

Office and truck

Share one route between iPhone, iPad, and Android so dispatch and the driver are looking at the same line.

Edit when the permit changes

Move the pins when the permit office changes the route. The line on the screen should match the paper.

Texas import is not a guess

On the $3.99 plan, upload a Texas TxPROS PDF or scan the QR. PermitPath imports that state route. It does not rebuild the corridor from the written directions.

One code, one device

A fleet code unlocks one phone or tablet under Settings, Subscription, Company access. Fleet Solutions is where the office buys those codes.
