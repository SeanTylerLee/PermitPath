Texas permit import

Upload a PDF of your Texas oversize permit or scan the QR code in PermitPath. The app imports the official permitted route from the TxPROS system—the route the State of Texas issued—so you can navigate that exact path. This is not AI guessing or rebuilding directions from the paper permit.

Texas import is one feature. In any state you can build the route manually. See how Texas permit import works, the Texas oversize permits guide, and the oversize loads guide. Want official import in another state? Send a permit from there.
