Getting the permit

Who issues Kentucky oversize permits

Vehicles and loads that exceed Kentucky legal size or weight limits need an oversize/overweight permit from Kentucky Transportation Cabinet (KYTC). The permit authorizes the move and sets the route the load is allowed to drive.

PermitPath is not a permit office and does not sell permits. Limits, fees, travel hours, and permit types change, so always confirm the details with KYTC before you move.
