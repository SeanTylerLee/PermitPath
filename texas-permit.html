Texas detail

How Texas TxPROS import works

Option 1

Upload the PDF

Use the original PDF of your Texas oversize permit. PermitPath reads the TxPROS permit from that file and imports the official route.

Option 2

Scan the QR code

Scan the QR code on the Texas permit with PermitPath. That pulls the same official TxPROS route into the app.

Walkthroughs for Texas import, manual routes, and company codes are on How It Works. Need background on Texas permits? Read the Texas oversize permits guide.

Official Texas route

Navigate the exact path Texas already issued

After a Texas import, you get the official permitted route from the TxPROS system. That is the route the State of Texas gave you—not a rebuilt version of the paper directions.

You can then navigate that exact permitted path in PermitPath. You do not have to worry about drifting onto a guessed line, because the line in the app is the official Texas route.

PermitPath does not sell or issue oversize permits. Texas permits come from the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles through TxDMV / TxPROS. After you have the permit, import puts the route in the cab.

What Texas import is

The official route. Not a guess.

This is

  • The official route from the State of Texas

  • The exact permitted path, ready to navigate
  • Started with a PDF upload or a QR scan

This is not

  • AI building or guessing a route

  • A shortcut that may put you off the permitted path
