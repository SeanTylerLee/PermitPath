How it works

Bring the permit in. Drive the official path.

Step 1

Import your permit

Upload the permit PDF or scan the QR code in PermitPath for a supported state. The app pulls in the route that state issued.

Step 2

Navigate the official route

Walkthroughs for import, manual routes, and company codes are on How It Works.

Official route

The path the state already issued

After import, you navigate the official permitted route from that state—not a rebuilt version of the paper directions, and not a guessed line.

Texas does this today. Wyoming, North Dakota, Colorado, Virginia, Georgia, and Kentucky release in the app on November 20 and will import the official route from that state the same way.

PermitPath does not sell or issue oversize permits. Those come from each state’s permit office. After you have the permit, import puts the official route in the cab.

What import is

The official route. Not a guess.

This is

  • The official route from the state that issued the permit
  • Texas in the app now, and six more states on November 20
  • The exact permitted path, ready to navigate
  • Started from your permit PDF or QR code

This is not

  • AI building or guessing a route
  • A line rebuilt from the written driving directions
  • Rebuilding turns from the paper and hoping they match
  • A shortcut that may put you off the permitted path

Always compare with your permit

The paper or PDF permit is the legal authority, not the app. Before you start, check the line in PermitPath against your permit, and stop following the app if they ever disagree. See the Terms of Use.
