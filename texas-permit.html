Upload a PDF of your Texas permit or scan the QR code in PermitPath. The app imports the official permitted route from TxPROS—the route the State of Texas gave you—so you can navigate it exactly.

How it works

Two ways to import your Texas permit

Option 1

Upload the PDF

Use the original PDF of your Texas oversize permit. PermitPath reads the TxPROS permit from that file and imports the official route.

Option 2

Scan the QR code

Scan the QR code on the Texas permit with PermitPath. That pulls the same official TxPROS route into the app.

Official route

Navigate the exact path Texas already issued

After import, you get the official permitted route from the TxPROS system. That is the route the State of Texas gave you—not a rebuilt version of the paper directions.

You can then navigate that exact permitted path in PermitPath. You do not have to worry about drifting onto a guessed line, because the line in the app is the official Texas route.

What this is

The official route. Not a guess.
